Admissions: campuses without a building stop offering tours

The thank-you page told every family "schedule your tour or meeting". The high
school and Virtual have no building to tour, and inviting somebody to walk
around a school that does not exist is the kind of small wrongness a family
notices and a school never hears about. They now offer to schedule a day and
time to meet virtually.

Driven by a column on the school, not a list of names in the code. The obvious
shortcut works until somebody renames a campus in the admin screen, at which
point the page silently starts offering tours again and nothing reports a
problem. Whether a campus meets families in person is a fact about the campus.

Defaults to false — a school that has not said otherwise has a front door — so
Florida and Georgia are unaffected.
