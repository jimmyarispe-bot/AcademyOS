Admissions: the inquiry thank-you says why we want to talk

Adds to both parent-facing inquiry emails:

  "We would love to hear what you are looking for in a learning environment for
   your child."

Two templates, two placements, because they say different things. The one with
the booking link puts it between "The next step is a conversation" and "Please
pick a time that suits you" — so the reason reaches the parent before the link,
rather than after it where people stop reading. The one without a link puts it
before "If it is easier, simply reply to this email", so an invitation to write
back follows a reason to write back.

Inserted with replace() rather than rewritten. These bodies were read through a
grid that truncates, so parts of them were inferred rather than seen, and
retyping a whole body would mean retyping sentences nobody has read in full.
An insertion anchored on a phrase that was read in full changes exactly what it
claims to and leaves everything else alone.

Guarded on the sentence not already being present, so it is re-runnable, and
the check at the end prints whether each template actually took it — 344
reported success while changing nothing, and that is the failure this pattern
is avoiding.
