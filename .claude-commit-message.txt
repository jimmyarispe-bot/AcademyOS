Admissions: money looks like money

A new `currency` field type. It renders as a number field with a dollar sign
inside it, on the left, where a parent copying a figure off an award letter can
see what the box wants while they are typing into it. A label reading
"amount ($)" is read once and then forgotten during the part that matters.

The stored value is unchanged — a plain number. The symbol is decoration and
nobody has to type or delete it.

THE VALIDATOR NOW READS WHAT PARENTS ACTUALLY TYPE. Number("$10,463.00") is NaN.
A parent copying that figure off the letter in front of them is reading
correctly, and telling them it is not a valid number is how a form loses a
family who did nothing wrong. Dollar signs, commas and spaces are stripped
before parsing. Five tests, including the one that keeps it honest: "about ten
thousand" is still rejected.

fl_scholarship_amount and ga_special_needs_amount become currency in interest
form v9. "GTID" becomes "GA Special Needs Scholarship GTID" — on its own the
acronym sat under a heading and left a parent to infer which of their
scholarships it belonged to.

The key stays ga_gtid. It moved in v7 an hour ago because nothing had been
answered under it, and that is still true — but a key should stop moving once it
is right.

SHIP BEFORE RUNNING THE MIGRATION. An unknown field type falls through to the
generic text input: it works, and it shows no symbol.
