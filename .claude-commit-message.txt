Parents: invitations, and the ninth GA GOAL eligibility route

PARENT INVITATIONS. Nothing here is new machinery. createManagedUser already
creates the auth user, delivers the invitation through Resend rather than
Supabase SMTP, and gates the account behind invite_activation until the person
sets a password at /login/activate. A PARENT role already exists with its own
permission group. None of it had ever been pointed at a guardian.

The one genuinely missing step is one line: setting guardians.user_id so the
account a parent activates connects to the family record the portal reads.
Without it a parent signs in successfully and sees nothing — the worst of the
three possible outcomes, because it looks like it worked. That write checks its
error, since an invitation that landed and a link that did not is the one
combination producing a confused family and no trace of why.

Not inviteManagedUsers: it invents a name from the email local part, so
katieallen315@yahoo.com becomes "Katieallen315 User". For staff typing addresses
into a box that is a fair guess; for guardians we hold real names.

THIS SENDS REAL EMAIL TO REAL FAMILIES, so the safeguards are the feature:
nothing is selected by default, the caller must type SEND, there is no
invite-everyone-everywhere path, one campus at a time, and a batch cap of 50 so
a mistake reaches fifty families rather than all of them. Eligibility is
re-checked at send time, not only in the preview — a list is minutes old by the
time somebody clicks, and inviting a parent twice sends a second set of
credentials for an account they already activated.

Skipped guardians are listed with the reason rather than filtered away. A list
that quietly omits half a campus is a list nobody can check.

CAUGHT BY THE REPOSITORY'S OWN CHECK: the client list imported its batch
constant from the module that imports the service-role client, which would have
dragged server-only code into the browser bundle. The constant now lives alone.
That check exists for exactly this and it earned its keep.

GA GOAL, ninth route: a scholarship already held from another Student
Scholarship Organization at a different private school. A child transferring
from another private school cannot satisfy "six weeks in a Georgia public school
immediately prior", so without this every transferring family reads as
ineligible. It comes from GOAL's own Submit a New Student guidance and NOT from
the statute text this form otherwise quotes verbatim; Georgia's SSO chapter
makes each organization solely responsible for verifying eligibility, so GOAL is
the authority on its own list. The wording describes the family's situation
rather than asserting a legal conclusion.
