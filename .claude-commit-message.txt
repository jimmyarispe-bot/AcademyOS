Platform: a press looks like a press, everywhere at once

Three signals now, driven from one place.

The bar goes from two pixels to four and sweeps instead of pulsing. At 2px on a
bright header it read as a border; movement is what reads as "working" from the
corner of the eye.

The whole page takes a waiting cursor. Setting `cursor` on <body> alone did
nothing useful — the cursor is decided by the element under the pointer, and
every link and button carries `cursor: pointer`, so the one person guaranteed
not to see it was the person hovering the thing they just clicked. A class plus
a rule covering every descendant fixes that.

And both are driven by GlobalProgressProvider rather than by each call site, so
every action that already reports progress gets them for free, as does every
navigation. Nothing to remember at the call site, and nothing to add to the
next button somebody writes — provided it uses the action system.

Known gap: about seventy client components run a mutation from a hand-rolled
button without going through useActionFeedback. Those still show nothing but
their own local state. They need finding and converting; this ship does not do
that.
