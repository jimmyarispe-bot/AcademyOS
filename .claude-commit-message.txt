Access: assigning schools sets them, and a school can be taken away

Nina Gaddy was assigned to The Academy GA and came out with GA and FL.

Three faults, compounding:

The dialog's checkboxes rendered unticked whatever the person already had, so
it showed "no schools" for somebody who had one. "Assign School" then upserted
each ticked box and removed nothing — ticking GA meant "also give them GA", not
"their schools are GA". And there was no removal path anywhere in the identity
layer: the only deletes wipe every assignment as part of deleting the user. So
school access could be granted and never revoked, from any screen.

On an access-control surface, a screen that shows one thing and does another is
the whole problem, not a rough edge.

Now: the boxes show what the person holds, the ticked set is the answer — added
if missing, removed if unticked — and removal exists as its own action against
both tables that record access, logged as a security event. Granting access is
routine; removing it is what somebody asks about six months later.

An empty selection means no school access, which an administrator needs to be
able to do. It reads as a decision rather than an accident because the dialog
now shows what they had before they submit.
