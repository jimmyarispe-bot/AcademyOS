Admissions: the Student Questionnaire panel actually renders

A case section lives in two places. The definition creates the tab and loads
its data; a static switch decides what to draw — static rather than a registry
lookup, because resolving a "use client" section through the runtime registry
breaks the RSC client boundary in a production build.

The Student Questionnaire was added to the definition and not the switch. The
tab appeared, loaded its data, and rendered "Section module is not registered":
a sentence no member of staff can act on, and one that reads as a broken
feature rather than a missing line.

Adds the case, and a test that reads both lists and fails when they disagree —
in either direction — so the next person is only reminded once.

Also in this ship: version 16, which cuts Virtual's programme list to the one
programme it runs.
